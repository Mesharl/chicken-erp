# Finance model

## Principles
- Track revenue from sales orders and payments
- Track cost from feed, purchases, labour and expenses
- Allocate costs to farm and flock where possible
- Prefer actual movements over estimates

## Key measures
- Revenue by period
- Cost of feed per flock
- Mortality loss exposure
- Gross margin on eggs and live birds
- Inventory valuation from movement costs

## Payments
Supported methods: cash, M-Pesa, bank, credit, other.
